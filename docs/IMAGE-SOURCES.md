# Image provenance

The clinic, treatment, gallery and product photographs were present in the original repository. No source URLs, author credits or image licences were supplied with them, and their reuse rights have not been verified. This project does not claim ownership of these photographs or grant a licence for them. Confirm permissions or replace them with appropriately licensed photographs before reusing them elsewhere.

Identifiable clinician headshots and the original logo are no longer displayed. Fictional therapist profiles use CSS initials avatars. Original image files remain in `assets/images/` to preserve the source material and Git history.

Bootstrap 5.0.2 is distributed under the MIT licence; the unmodified local distribution and its licence are in `assets/vendor/`.

The screenshots in `docs/screenshots/` are actual Chromium captures of this website. They include the existing photographs and do not establish reuse rights for those photographs.
